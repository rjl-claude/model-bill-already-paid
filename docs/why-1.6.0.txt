Richard's ruling of 19 Nov 2026 (report-series ADR 0264, aios-harness#247): the part of the generators' share of grid (TNUoS) and balancing (BSUoS) charges that stations with no subsidy pay is counted, above the gas-led alternative, as its own indirect line, labelled estimated (`generators_share`). Every figure that holds all lines moves with it: the VAT line (5% of households' share of the new line), the indirect layer and all lines each year 2002-2025 and in the headline, the headline's modelled and estimated parts, share of the bill, every per-household and own-bills figure, the headline's range and counting options (the case without grid and balancing takes the new line out with them), and the bridge with Report No. 1 (its two ends, its VAT step and its power step, which hands No. 1's generators' share to a new step). No other line moves.
